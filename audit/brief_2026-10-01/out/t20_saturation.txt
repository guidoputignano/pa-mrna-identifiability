  (2) WHY THE INTEGRAL ESTIMATORS COLLAPSE ON TB-A.
      (b) 'saturated' assumes g = 1, so theta enters only as L0 - theta and
      is structurally unidentifiable. (b')/(b'') use the proper
      g = m/(K_m+m), but theta's regressor is INT g: it is identifiable only
      when g VARIES across the record. When g is small and nearly flat, the
      INT g column is collinear with the constant (tau) and INT m columns.
      TB-A (theta=150): g spans 0.207-0.351 (range 0.144); design cond[tau,∫g,∫m] = 2.1e+04;
             noiseless (b') theta-hat = 153.5 against truth 150  (RECOVERED)
      TB-B (theta=600): g spans 0.137-0.748 (range 0.611); design cond[tau,∫g,∫m] = 3.2e+03;
             noiseless (b') theta-hat = 599.6 against truth 600  (RECOVERED)
      NUANCE (correcting the brief's framing): (b')/(b'') are NOT g=1-
      misspecified -- they use the proper g and RECOVER theta noiselessly even
      on TB-A. Their TB-A collapse is ILL-CONDITIONING: cond ~2e4, so the 5%
      assay noise is amplified ~2e4-fold and theta blows up. g is nearly flat
      (0.21-0.35), so the ∫g column barely separates. On TB-B the excursion
      sweeps g to 0.75, cond falls ~7x, and theta survives the noise. Only (b)
      assumes g=1 (a ~1/0.35 ~ 3 misspecification at 5%), and it cannot
      identify theta regardless. So: ill-conditioning from sub-saturation, not
      a misspecified flux, is what sinks the integral estimators on TB-A.

      theta becomes identifiable (sd and cond both fall) as the excursion
      drives the peak g UP from its sub-saturated baseline ~0.35. Clearing
      0.20 needs f ~ 2.3 (peak g ~ 0.57, marker ~131, well above K_m=100) --
      the enzyme pushed toward saturation, though not all the way to g=1. That
      f ~ 2.3 is ~2x past the 1.177x admissible edge. So 'needs an excursion'
      and 'needs to drive the enzyme toward saturation' are ONE constraint:
      theta's integral regressor ∫g only separates when g is swept upward, and
      sweeping it means adding substrate past the crisis fold. The dynamic
      route fails for the same reason it would be unsafe.
