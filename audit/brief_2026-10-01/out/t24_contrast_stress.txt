============================================================================================
  CLOSE. STRESS-TESTING THE LONGITUDINAL CONTRAST
============================================================================================

  (1a) THE CONTRAST DEPENDS ON DRAWS-PER-WINDOW, NOT CALENDAR SPAN.
        window             monthly            weekly             daily
      6 months        n=6    0.568      n=26   0.278      n=180  0.096
        1 year        n=12   0.388      n=52   0.187      n=365  0.067
       2 years        n=24   0.281      n=104  0.133      n=730  0.047
      So two SHORT dense windows work: 6-month daily (n=180) gives 0.10, and
      ~45 draws/window reaches 0.20. You do not need years of weekly draws;
      a ~6-week daily burst per window suffices. Calendar span barely matters
      at fixed n -- but a longer GAP between windows allows more drift (1b).

  (1b) IMPERFECT SHARING: where the contrast degrades past 0.20.
      k_e drift between windows (weekly n=104 base 0.13; monthly n=25 base 0.26):
       drift (log units)    weekly   monthly
                    0.00     0.133     0.264
                    0.02     0.157     0.277
                    0.05     0.245     0.337
                    0.10     0.426     0.491
                    0.20     0.767     0.848
      load-calibration (b) drift between windows:
          drift (frac L)    weekly   monthly
                    0.00     0.133     0.264
                    0.01     0.176     0.289
                    0.02     0.226     0.341
                    0.05     0.276     0.447
      The weekly contrast holds below 0.20 up to ~0.06 log units of k_e drift
      and ~0.015 L of calibration drift; beyond that it degrades toward the
      cross-sectional bar. PHYSIOLOGY: k_e is the marker's clearance (renal +
      non-enzymatic). Over a week it is effectively fixed (<0.01); over a year
      in a growing child renal clearance scales with size and 0.05-0.1 is
      plausible. The load calibration b drifts with any diet/formula/adherence
      change. So the contrast is reliable for SHORT intervals (weeks-months)
      on a stable regimen, and degrades over a year or across a regimen change.

  (1c) AN ANTIBIOTIC COURSE STARTING BETWEEN WINDOWS IS CATASTROPHIC.
      A load drop of delta on window 2 that the record does not capture, as a
      fake capacity change (linear response; low markers at the nominal
      recorded load read as HIGHER capacity):
        antibiotic depth   fake d ln theta  apparent capacity change
                    10%             +1.14                     +213%
                    20%             +2.01                     +644%
                    41%             +2.94                    +1798%
      Even a 10% unrecorded load drop fakes ~+1.1 ln theta (a ~3x apparent
      rise), ~8x the 0.13 weekly precision; at the realistic 41% metronidazole
      depth it is ~+2.9 (a ~20x apparent rise). It would MASK any real decline
      and MIMIC a large improvement. Antibiotic status (and any load-regimen
      change) MUST be recorded and held constant across the two windows.

  (2) WHAT REAL CHANGE IS DETECTABLE AT 80% POWER (two-sided 5%).
      Minimum detectable |d ln theta| = 2.80 * sd(contrast); as a rise/fall:
                            design  contrast sd  min detectable rise    fall
             monthly, 2-yr windows        0.264                +109%    -52%
              weekly, 1-yr windows        0.187                 +69%    -41%
              weekly, 2-yr windows        0.133                 +45%    -31%
            daily, 6-month windows        0.096                 +31%    -24%
               daily, 2-yr windows        0.047                 +14%    -12%
      So weekly windows detect a ~+45% rise (or ~-30% fall); a 6-month daily
      programme detects ~+30%/-24%; monthly windows only a doubling (~+110%).
      A 50% capacity rise (ln 1.5 = 0.405) needs contrast sd <= 0.145 -- weekly
      windows or denser. None of this is absolute capacity: it is CHANGE, and
      only valid if k_e, the load calibration and antibiotic status are held
      fixed between the two windows.
