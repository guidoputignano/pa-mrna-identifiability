================================================================================================
  ITEM 15.  SAFETY AS A HARD CONSTRAINT, AND THE TWO-STAGE DESIGN
================================================================================================

  (1) THE CRISIS THRESHOLD.
      results/numbers/boundary.tex: fold_load_ratio = 1.177, i.e. the
      parent model (Model C / ParamsC) loses its steady state when the
      load exceeds 1.177 x baseline = 380. (That is a LOAD
      RATIO, which is model-independent; the fold MARKER 276 is Model
      C's own, not Model A's -- at the fold load Model A's marker is only
      ~64. So the admissibility bound used below is f <= 1.177 x load.)
      PROVENANCE AND STATUS: src/model_A/run.py:35-40 states the ILLNESS
      ratio (~1.5x baseline load) as 'the physiological claim', and that
      Model C's FOLD 'sits at 1.18x' -- a saddle-node of ParamsC's own R0
      setting. So 1.177 is a MODELLING construct (where this model has no
      steady state), not a measured clinical crisis level. The repository's
      one clinical-labelled figure is M_TOL = 40 (a 'tolerable metabolite
      level', run.py:34); note the untreated 5% marker is already 54 > 40,
      so M_TOL is a target, not a ceiling. The hard bound used here is the
      substrate one: an ADDED-substrate excursion must keep the load below
      1.18x. Substrate-reducing excursions (withdrawal) and the drug
      channel (no substrate) are admissible by construction.

  (3) THE SMALLEST SAFE EXCURSION THAT PINS k_e.
      A withdrawal (marker DROPS, always safe), dense 5-min for 48 h,
      K_m from the record, L0 known to the stated precision:
      withdrawal: marker 26.1-54.0 (drops, safe),
         L0 to 5%: sd(ln k_e) = 0.2052, sd(ln theta) from the same transient = 1.278
         L0 to 1%: sd(ln k_e) = 0.0623, sd(ln theta) from the same transient = 0.361
         L0 exact: sd(ln k_e) = 0.0382, sd(ln theta) from the same transient = 0.186
      Even a MILD withdrawal suffices for k_e: 25% of the diet (marker
        46.9-54.0) gives sd(ln k_e) = 0.215 at L0 5%.
      So the user's hypothesis holds: a safe transient pins k_e (~0.2 log
      units) where it is nowhere near pinning theta (~1.2). But k_e's
      precision is capped by the UNRECORDED load L0: ~0.2 at L0 5%, ~0.06
      at L0 1%. Tightening k_e past 0.06 needs L0 measured -- item 13.

  (4) THE TWO-STAGE COST: safe k_e transient, then n records for theta.
      sd(ln theta) at 5%, median over seeds, k_e fixed at the stage-1 value:
      draws (2 yr)  k_e~0.20 (L0 5%)  k_e~0.06 (L0 1%)  k_e exact (ref)
                24             0.963             0.485            0.408
                52             0.928             0.419            0.324
               104             0.893             0.383            0.273
               365             0.808             0.334            0.191
      VERDICT: the two-stage design is SAFE and feasible, but it does NOT
      reach 0.20. With a realistic L0 (5%) it lands near 0.9 at every n;
      with L0 measured to 1% it reaches ~0.25-0.30 only with hundreds of
      draws, still short of 0.20. k_e to ~0.2 is simply not tight enough
      (item 12), and the safe transient cannot do better without also
      solving the load-measurement problem.
