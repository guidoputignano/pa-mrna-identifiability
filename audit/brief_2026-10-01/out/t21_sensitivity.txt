  (1) DOES 'NO FEASIBLE POINT REACHES 0.20' SURVIVE PARAMETER VARIATION?
      sd(ln theta) at 5%, k_e to 0.2, daily draws over 2 yr (730). Baseline:
      baseline (K_m 100, assay 5%, k_e prior 0.8, perdraw): 0.716
                               variation  sd(ln theta)   > 0.20?
                                K_m = 50         0.342       yes

                             assay CV 3%         0.661       yes
                             assay CV 8%         0.787       yes

      CONCLUSION: the sd stays FAR above 0.20 under every variation except
      shrinking the achievable k_e precision itself (0.05 -> ~0.25, still >0.20).
      'No feasible point reaches 0.20' survives; it is governed by k_e precision
      (item 12), not by K_m, L, the noise model or the assay CV.

  (2) DOES THE FLUX 0.20 CONTOUR (~19% AT 5%) SURVIVE?
      single-reading flux crossing Y*, median over seeds:
                           variation  Y* crossing
      baseline (5%, K_m100, assay5%)        19.3%

                         assay CV 8%        18.8%
      The contour is stable in the ~16-22% band: the flux route's requirement
      (an absolutely-calibrated reading to ~1 part in 5) is robust.

  (3) THE FLUX CONTOUR ACROSS CAPACITY (the deliverable, extended).
      single-reading flux crossing Y* at which sd(ln theta) = 0.20:
       capacity  Y* (one reading)  Y* (3 readings)
            1%             19.4%            33.9%
            2%             19.4%            33.9%
            5%             19.3%            33.8%
           10%             19.3%            33.8%
           20%             19.5%            34.2%
           50%             23.6%            41.4%
          100%             60.0%            90.0%
      Y* is the LOOSEST reading that still reaches 0.20 (higher = easier).
      So: a single calibrated flux reading to ~19% recovers theta to 0.20
      across the clinically relevant low-capacity range (~1-20%), where the
      requirement is essentially FLAT at ~19%. It only LOOSENS above ~50%
      (Y* 24% at 50%, >60% at 100%), because there the monthly record ALONE
      already approaches 0.20 (Task 1's U-curve). The reading matters most
      exactly where capacity is lowest -- which is where it is needed.

  (4) PROVENANCE OF 'k_e ACHIEVABLE TO ~0.2 LOG UNITS'.
      It is NOT an external measurement capability; it is what item 15's SAFE
      within-fold transient delivers, and it is set by the unrecorded load L0:
          L0 to 5% (the repo load prior): safe transient gives sd(ln k_e) = 0.205
                                L0 to 2%: safe transient gives sd(ln k_e) = 0.103
                                L0 to 1%: safe transient gives sd(ln k_e) = 0.062
                                L0 exact: safe transient gives sd(ln k_e) = 0.038
      So 'k_e to 0.2' = L0 known to 5% (the repository's own load prior). The
      0.20 theta-contour needs k_e to ~0.05, which needs L0 to ~1%. The whole
      question is that gap: knowing the UNRECORDED load to 5% vs 1%.
