      the 5x5 FIM equals a single calibrated reading at Y_eff = hypot(Y, Z): worst gap over the grid 2.8e-16

      => 0.20 CONTOUR:  hypot(Y, Z) <= 19.3% (FIM) / 18.4% (Monte Carlo).
      REQUIREMENT SENTENCE: an observable proportional to the enzymatic
      flux, read to Y% with calibration known to Z%, reaches sd(ln theta)
      = 0.20 at 5% of normal if and only if sqrt(Y^2 + Z^2) <= ~18-19%.
      (Averaging n readings divides Y by sqrt(n) but NOT Z: a single
       shared calibration c is the floor. Three readings at Y need
       Z <= 19.3% on its own to pass.)

  (2) REPARAMETERISATION: fraction oxidised s = theta g(m)/L, with L.
      (s to Y%, L to X%); flux = s*L. sd(ln theta), median over seeds:
        X \ Y      5.0%  10.0%  15.0%  17.5%  20.0%  25.0%
      L exact   0.065 0.108 0.155 0.179 0.203 0.251
      L    1%   0.065 0.108 0.155 0.179 0.203 0.251
      L    2%   0.065 0.108 0.155 0.179 0.203 0.251
      L    5%   0.067 0.109 0.156 0.180 0.204 0.252
      0.20 crossing in Y, per load precision X:
         L exact: s to Y <= 19.69%   (hypot(Y,X) = 19.7%)
         L to 1%: s to Y <= 19.69%   (hypot(Y,X) = 19.7%)
         L to 2%: s to Y <= 19.68%   (hypot(Y,X) = 19.8%)
         L to 5%: s to Y <= 19.60%   (hypot(Y,X) = 20.2%)
      So the (s, L) pair reaches target on the SAME locus: s and L
      enter as ln(flux) = ln s + ln L, so a load measurement to X% plays
      exactly the role of calibrating c to Z%. There is no free lunch in
      switching to the fraction oxidised; it only moves the calibration
      burden onto an independent measurement of L.

  (3) WHAT THE REPOSITORY'S BREATH TEST CAN AND CANNOT DELIVER.
      Finding (round 2): u2_breath_functional.py models it as y = c*F(m),
      F = theta g/(theta g + k_e m) = the fraction oxidised = s, with the
      recovery c UNKNOWN. On the specification above:
      - It can deliver the fraction oxidised s (a relative reading), i.e.
        a Y. It CANNOT deliver the absolute flux: c is its unknown Z.
      - With c entirely unknown (Z -> inf), one reading is worth nothing
        (t12): sqrt(Y^2 + Z^2) -> inf, off the contour.
      - It reaches target ONLY if BOTH (i) its recovery c is calibrated to
        Z <= ~18% AND (ii) the load L is measured to X, with hypot of the
        reading's Y, the calibration Z and the load X <= ~18-19%.
      Whether such a calibration exists is a domain question, not answered
      here. No other assay is assumed.
