============================================================================================
  ADD-ON 1&2. SAMPLING AT REALISTIC k_e, AND LONGITUDINAL PRECISION
============================================================================================

  (1) sd(ln theta) AT 5% vs SAMPLING, with sd(ln k_e) = 0.06 (feasible),
      over a two-year programme (median over 24 seeds):
                    protocol  draws/2yr   k_e=0.06  k_e exact (ref)
               monthly (~25)         25      0.465            0.379
           fortnightly (~52)         52      0.419            0.324
               weekly (~104)        104      0.383            0.273
            ~daily/2d (~365)        365      0.334            0.191
                daily (~730)        730      0.310            0.149
      The k_e = 0.06 floor (|beta|*0.06 ~ 0.30) is approached by daily; monthly
      sits above it at ~0.47, so sampling DOES buy ~1/3 (0.47 -> 0.31). None
      reaches 0.20: a feasible monthly protocol lands at ~0.47, weekly ~0.38.

  (2) LONGITUDINAL CONTRAST sd(ln theta_2 - ln theta_1), ONE patient.
      The shared k_e, load-bias b and K_m cancel in the difference, so the
      contrast is limited only by the INDEPENDENT per-visit noise -- far below
      the cross-sectional bar. (median over seed pairs; k_e prior 0.8 as usual)
       draws per visit  sd(d ln theta)  cross-sectional sd(ln theta)
                    25           0.264                         2.123
                    52           0.191                         1.883
                   104           0.133                         1.529
                   365           0.067                         0.972
      So monitoring a CHANGE is 0.26 (monthly) / 0.13 (weekly) -- the weekly
      contrast is BELOW 0.20, which no cross-sectional design reaches. This is
      the one regime where the marker is clinically usable at target precision.

  (2b) BUT ONLY IF k_e AND THE LOAD CALIBRATION ARE STABLE BETWEEN VISITS.
      The cancellation is exact only if the shared nuisances do not drift. The
      'interval between estimates' enters ONLY through drift (with perfect
      sharing the contrast is interval-independent). Sensitivity, n=25/visit:
       k_e drift (log units)  sd(d ln theta)
                        0.00           0.264
                        0.02           0.277
                        0.05           0.337
                        0.10           0.491
                        0.20           0.848
      load-bias drift (frac L)  sd(d ln theta)
                        0.00           0.264
                        0.02           0.341
                        0.05           0.447
                        0.10           0.496
      The advantage survives drift up to ~0.05 log units in k_e (contrast
      ~0.34); beyond that it erodes toward the cross-sectional value. Whether
      k_e is that stable over the monitoring interval is a clinical assumption,
      not something the repository can settle.
