## C2 — not a double count. But the correction costs a rank.

### The derivation, stated explicitly

A record is a set of **pairs** (m_i, s_i). The model asserts a balance between
them, `s_i = (θ + Δ_i) g(m_i;K_m) + k_e m_i + b + ε_i`, with Δ_i a **known
regressor** read off the dosing record, not a parameter. The Fisher column is the
derivative at **fixed m_i**, because m_i is the abscissa — data, not model
output. Δ enters in two different roles: it moves the abscissa (through the
data-generating process, `quasi_steady_level`) and it scales the K_m coefficient
(through the model). Neither substitutes for the other. Dropping it does not
remove a duplicate; it linearises the likelihood about a model the record was
not generated from.

### What the published column was actually hiding

Not a duplicate — **a null direction.** With Δ carried and the recorded load
constant, the three columns are *exactly* rank deficient. Proved symbolically
(`sympy` returns 0 identically) and numerically (‖Qv‖/‖Q‖ = 5.8×10⁻¹⁷):

```
c_theta * q_theta + c_Km * q_Km = q_b ,
c_theta = (1 + K_m k_e / L)/theta ,   c_Km = -1/L
```

The load-bias column lies in the span of the other two. Eigenvalues of the
prior-free information matrix, dense design: published `[2.3e-2, 3.7, 4.5e4]`;
corrected `[1.6e-5, 1.6e3, 8.0e4]`. The correction converts the second direction
from eigenvalue 3.7 to 1624 — a factor of 437 in information — at the cost of
making the third exactly null.

So `sd = 0.0251` is filled in by the **load-record prior**, not by the data. The
null direction is identified only at second order: the noiseless χ² along it goes
as ε⁴ exactly (χ²/ε⁴ constant at 2.08×10⁻⁶ over four decades of ε).

### The degeneracy is an artefact of the synthetic design's constant load

The identity needs `c_Km = −1/L_i` to be the same constant for every sample. Any
variation in recorded intake breaks it, and does so immediately:

| day-to-day load CV | prior-free, Δ in | with the 5% prior |
|---|---|---|
| 0% (as coded) | **SINGULAR** | 0.0251 |
| 2% | 0.0539 | 0.0249 |
| 5% | 0.0305 | 0.0243 |
| 10% | 0.0229 | 0.0223 |
| 40% | 0.0195 | 0.0195 |

A real record breaks the degeneracy without needing the prior at all. This is
the load-record sweep 2B.1 asked for, arrived at from the other side — and it
says the opposite of 2B.1's premise: **load-record precision is nearly
irrelevant once Δ is carried.** On the footing A1 recommends (k_e fitted),
sd(ln θ) runs 0.1469 at a 1% load record to 0.1557 at 20%.

### Monte Carlo

MC of the same nonlinear estimator, dense design: **0.0206–0.0233** across five
seeds against the Fisher's 0.0251, start-dispersion independent (0.0, 0.1, 0.5,
1.0 all agree). The Fisher does not overstate information — the ratio does not
exceed 1 — so nothing is being counted twice.

## C3 — SPLIT. The precision gain is robust. The correlation flip is not.

**Robust.** Across every sweep — operating point m̄/K_m from 0.02 to 10, θ from
300 to 30 000, k_e from 0.5 to 50, dose amplitude Δ/θ from 0.2 to 4, dose
interval 7–28 d, sampling 1–7 d, record 14–730 d — carrying Δ improves
sd(ln θ), on both footings, without exception.

**Not robust.** `corr(ln θ, ln K_m) = −0.3163` is a cancellation between the
prior-fed null direction (which contributes negative correlation) and the
identified subspace (positive). It is:

- **+0.0214**, not −0.3163, on the footing A1 recommends (k_e fitted);
- sign-labile across the sweep — −0.67 at k_e = 50, **+0.70** at D_AMP = 600,
  **+0.81** on a single dose cycle;
- **+0.95 in Monte Carlo**, against the Fisher's −0.32, at every seed.

**Do not put the correlation flip in the paper.** What is true and robust is
that the correlation *collapses* in magnitude, from +0.96 to near zero.

### A methodological finding that belongs in the paper

The information filter of `eq:infofilter` is a first-order object. Once Δ is
carried it is describing a design matrix that is exactly rank deficient, and it
reports the null direction as prior-held when it is in fact curvature-held. The
two happen to agree in magnitude here (prior 95% half-width ±31.6 in b against
the profile-likelihood ±36.9), so `0.0251` survives — but it survives for a
reason the filter does not contain. This is the same "delta method against
simulation" gap the paper already documents in `rem:no_delta`, in a second place.

the k_e-known footing and must not go in the abstract.

Δ as a difference, so the patient CV of 12.8% enters as 12.8·√2 = **18.1%**,
which **fails** at sd(ln θ) = 0.2119. Two pairs averaged returns 12.8% and
**passes** at 0.1840; four pairs give 9.1% and 0.1672. **The specification needs
at least two paired breath tests per patient, and that is a derived number, not
a recommendation.** (2D.1 owns turning this into a protocol; it is recorded here
because it changes what 2D.8 can ask for.)

**2B.1 is retired for a second reason.** Load-record precision was already
