\section{Was a genetic prior helpful?}

\Q Can genotype supply what the record cannot, as a prior on $\theta$ or an anchor for $k_e$?

\D Read §S1.10, Proposition 6 (§S1.18.1), Remark 14 (§S1.16.3), Remarks 21 and 22 (§S1.16.7),
Table~\rn{new.tab.keprior} (§\rn{new.sec.tier2}); recomputed the crossing from the model
constants; searched for a genotype prior on $\theta$.

\F \textbf{What the paper says}, identical in both drafts.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Ideal for the map, closed to the design} (§S1.10). \emph{PCCA} c.425G$>$A in
$\rn{lnew.saudi_founder}\%$ of families, one compound-heterozygous, so genotype to capacity is
unconfounded; series $\rn{lnew.saudi_pcca}\%$ \emph{PCCA}, \rn{lnew.saudi_pcca_n} patients.
But §\rn{new.sec.applic} needs a varying increment, which no patient outside the trial gets.
\item \textbf{The null-genotype anchor inverts with severity} (Remarks 21 and 22). $\theta =
0$ gives $s = k_e m$, which collapses the fibre to a point. Its load-error amplification
crosses one at $\theta = 2k_e(K_m+\bar m)$, $\rn{calc.null_cross}$ here (paper:
$\approx\rn{lnew.null_cross}$), and is $\rn{calc.null_amp300}$ at the parent's
$\theta = \rn{lnew.theta_parent}$ (paper: $\rn{lnew.null_amp}$).
\item \textbf{A known $k_e$ does not close the gate} (Table~\rn{new.tab.keprior}). Tightening
the published prior to $\rn{lnew.ke_tight}\%$ buys $\rn{lnew.ke_gain_nom}\times$ at nominal
capacity and $\rn{lnew.ke_gain_dis}\times$ at $\rn{calc.dis_pct}\%$ of it; an exact one leaves
$\rn{lnew.ke_exact_left}$ against the $\rn{calc.gate}$ gate.
\end{itemize}
\noindent A genotype-informed prior on $\theta$ itself: \nf, in either draft.
