# Literature reviews supplied September 2026, stored verbatim

**Nothing in this directory is a finding of this repository.** These are five review documents
supplied by the author in September 2026 and stored here **exactly as received**, including
their own errors, their own confidence labels and their own caveats. They are raw input.

**Do not cite anything from these files.** They are not sources. They are summaries of sources,
several of which turned out to be wrong. What survived checking lives in
`audit/PHASE6_REVIEWS_SEP2026.md`; what was registered lives in `paper/refs.sources`.

## The files

| file | subject |
|---|---|
| `01_3hp_timescales.md` | how fast plasma organic-acid markers respond to a change in capacity or load |
| `02_prior_art_ABC.md` | periodic dosing as a perturbation, sampling-time design, diet-input PK/PD models |
| `03_nondietary_propionate.md` | non-dietary propionate sources, quantified |
| `04_diet_variability.md` | dietary protein intake variability and plasma biomarkers |
| `05_breath_test_repeatability.md` | repeatability of 13C breath tests, and the 12% question |

The 3-HP review arrived **twice, verbatim**, in the same message. It is stored once.

## What checking found, in one paragraph

Three of the five state that their own retrieval tools failed and that they were written from
memory with confidence labels. **Seven citation errors were found**, including a DOI that
resolves to an unrelated neuroscience paper, a design paper attributed to the wrong author, and
the Manoli breath-test CV that `REVIEW_LOG.md` R4 had already retracted, repeated a second time.
Several claims were **wrong against the primary source**, notably the odd-chain fatty-acid
fraction, where this repository's existing figure was right and the review's was off by about a
factor of five. Other claims checked out exactly and closed open items. The per-claim verdicts
are in `audit/PHASE6_REVIEWS_SEP2026.md` sections 2, 5, 6 and 7.

**Read the assessment before using anything here.**
