# Marker-panel literature review for PA system identification: within-patient variability, functional forms, and the case for a second, independent marker

## TL;DR
- No study reporting a total within-patient CV for plasma 3-HP in propionic acidemia (PA) was found. The closest defensible primary numbers are between-visit serum MMA CV of 31.38 ± 24.55% in MMA (Manoli 2021, not PA, not 3-HP) and within-individual C3 propionylcarnitine daily CV of 26.4% in healthy adults (Thompson 2012). Both are far above the 5% analytical figure the design assumes, so the design's variability input is almost certainly optimistic by a factor of roughly 5 to 6.
- The panel is not four scaled copies of one pool. 3-HP is a near-linear overflow of propionyl-CoA, but 2-methylcitrate (2-MC) formation depends on a second substrate (oxaloacetate) via citrate synthase, C3/C2 is a near-equilibrium ratio set by carnitine acetyltransferase and therefore tracks the propionyl-CoA/acetyl-CoA ratio rather than the propionyl-CoA pool, and propionylglycine is made by glycine N-acyltransferase (GLYAT), a human enzyme with sigmoidal kinetics that depends on glycine. These are genuinely different functional forms.
- Recommendation: add the C3/C2 acylcarnitine ratio as the second marker, because its near-equilibrium ratio form carries information about the propionyl-CoA/acetyl-CoA ratio that a monotone 3-HP overflow marker cannot, and hold 2-MC as the secondary candidate because its dependence on oxaloacetate (a distinct TCA state) gives it a bilinear form. Both were measured in the trial and in the Pcca mouse, so no new assay is required.

## Key Findings

### 1. Within-patient variability: what exists and what does not
- **No 3-HP within-patient variability figure exists (NOT FOUND).** Neither the PA natural history study (Orphanet J Rare Dis 2026;21:152, DOI 10.1186/s13023-026-04251-3), the phase 1/2 trial (Nature 2024;628:872-877, DOI 10.1038/s41586-024-07266-7), nor the Longo biomarker review (J Inherit Metab Dis 2022, DOI 10.1002/jimd.12478) reports a same-day, day-to-day, or between-visit CV for 3-HP in PA in any matrix. The natural history paper explicitly notes "small sample sizes and high variability" for plasma 3-HP, C3, and 2-MC by age\[1\] but publishes no CV.
- **Best available proxy 1 (SECONDARY, MMA not PA):** Manoli 2021 (Genet Med 2021;23:1522-1533, DOI 10.1038/s41436-021-01143-8, PMID 33820958, PMC8354855) reports, for serum methylmalonic acid on repeat testing at intervals of 2 months to 4 years, that "CV for serum methylmalonic acid concentrations in the same subjects undergoing repeat testing was more variable, ranging from 1.5% to 75.3% with an average of 31.38 ± 24.55%."\[2\] This is a between-visit within-patient CV for a primary organic-acid marker in the sister disease, and it is the single most relevant number for calibrating a realistic marker CV.
- **Best available proxy 2 (SECONDARY, healthy adults not PA):** Thompson 2012 (Metabolomics 2012;8:556-565, DOI 10.1007/s11306-011-0345-9, PMID 25067934, PMC4107907) reports for propionylcarnitine (C3) a "Mean %CV (Range) Within Individuals" of "26.4 (8.2-61.1)", with between-individual %CV 39.2 and analytical %CV 7.9; for acetylcarnitine (C2) within-individual %CV was 20.5 (3.8-52.5).\[3\] These are daily (within-subject) biological CVs in serum from healthy adults, driven by feeding and activity.
- **Do NOT import the breath-test 12.8%.** Verified against the source: Manoli 2021 reports "Test repeatability was robust in controls and in MMA subjects (mean coefficient of variation 6.9% and 12.8%, respectively), despite widely variable serum methylmalonic acid concentrations in the patients." In the source the 12.8% is the 1-13C-propionate breath-test repeatability CV (a mean of per-subject CVs; the ±12.86% quoted elsewhere in the paper is the SD of that distribution), not a within-patient CV of any plasma marker, and it belongs to a different assay (breath, not plasma organic acid). It should not be used as a marker CV.
- **Implication for the design.** The robustness review's ladder (11 draws at 5%, 23 at 10%, 45 at 15%, unreachable in five years at 20%) means that at the empirically supported 25 to 35% range, monthly sampling does not identify theta within five years. The 5% assumption is analytical only and is not defensible as a total within-patient figure.

### 2. Formation pathway, enzyme, kinetic form, and confounders (per marker)

**3-Hydroxypropionate (3-HP).** Formed from propionyl-CoA by a modified beta-oxidation ("propionyl-CoA overflow") route: propionyl-CoA -> acrylyl-CoA -> 3-hydroxypropionyl-CoA -> 3-HP.\[4\] Confirmed in PA and MMA patients in vivo by Ando/Sweetman (PNAS 1972;69:2807, DOI 10.1073/pnas.69.10.2807): "The appearance of radioactive 3-hydroxypropionate in the urine after the administration of these compounds indicates that beta-oxidation of propionyl-CoA through acryloyl-CoA was functioning in these patients."\[5\] The rat-liver study of Kombu/Brunengraber (Am J Physiol Endocrinol Metab 2017, DOI 10.1152/ajpendo.00105.2017) states: "the mechanism of formation of 3HP involves reduction of propionyl-CoA to acrylyl-CoA, followed by hydration of acrylyl-CoA to 3HP-CoA and hydrolysis to 3HP."\[4\] **Functional form: essentially first-order/linear overflow of the propionyl-CoA pool, no known saturation at pathological concentrations (SECONDARY on linearity, VERIFIED on pathway).** Confounders: peripheral (extra-hepatic) amino-acid catabolism contributes circulating 3-HP; the rat study notes "3HP, derived from amino acid catabolism in peripheral tissues, is released in plasma,"\[4\] so 3-HP is not purely liver-derived.

**2-Methylcitrate (2-MC).** Formed by condensation of propionyl-CoA with oxaloacetate. In humans this is catalyzed by citrate synthase acting on propionyl-CoA as an alternative substrate (there is no separate human 2-methylcitrate synthase gene). Nikou/Venditti-adjacent primary work states 2-MC "is produced from the condensation of propionyl CoA with Krebs cycle intermediate oxaloacetate in a reaction thought to be catalyzed by citrate synthase"\[6\] (Chin Chim Acta / Sci Rep 2019, DOI 10.1038/s41598-019-48885-9). Kinetic anchors: human citrate synthase has very low Km for its native substrates (acetyl-CoA about 0.4 µM, oxaloacetate about 0.25 µM in human heart),\[7\] and fungal/eukaryotic methylcitrate synthase has Km for propionyl-CoA 1.7 µM and for oxaloacetate 0.63 µM (Aspergillus nidulans, Mol Microbiol 2000, DOI 10.1046/j.1365-2958.2000.01737.x). **Functional form: bilinear/saturating in TWO substrates (propionyl-CoA and oxaloacetate), so 2-MC depends on TCA/anaplerotic state, not on the propionyl-CoA pool alone (VERIFIED as a distinct form).** Confounders: oxaloacetate availability and TCA flux; the pyruvate-carboxylase knockout work (Cell Rep 2026, DOI in pending table) shows 2-MC is the one metabolite NOT exacerbated when anaplerosis is removed, confirming its oxaloacetate dependence.\[8\]

**Propionylcarnitine (C3).** Formed from propionyl-CoA and free carnitine by carnitine acyltransferase activity (carnitine acetyltransferase, CrAT, EC 2.3.1.7, handles acyl groups up to C4; Pharmacol Rev 2024, DOI 10.1124/pharmrev... in pending table: "Catalyzes the reversible transfer of acyl groups (up to C4) from carnitine to CoA").\[9\] **Functional form: near-equilibrium exchange, so C3 tracks the propionyl-CoA pool but is scaled by free-carnitine availability.** Confounders: free carnitine (C0) status, which is routinely low in PA and is therapeutically supplemented, directly rescales C3; feeding/fasting shifts C3 sharply.

**C3/C2 acylcarnitine ratio.** See Section 3. **Functional form: a near-equilibrium ratio reporting the mitochondrial propionyl-CoA/acetyl-CoA ratio, which is mathematically distinct from any single-pool marker.**

**Propionylglycine (n-PG).** Formed by conjugation of propionyl-CoA with glycine by glycine N-acyltransferase (GLYAT, EC 2.3.1.13). Human GLYAT kinetics are sigmoidal, not Michaelis-Menten: the primary GLYAT paper (van der Sluis et al., Metabolomics/Meta Gene, in pending table) states "Human GLYAT exhibits sigmoidal kinetics whereas bovine GLYAT follow Michaelis-Menten kinetics"\[10\] and that "GLYAT is a sigmoidal enzyme, which means that reaction kinetics are influenced by the concentration of both substrates used."\[10\] **Functional form: sigmoidal (cooperative) in acyl-CoA and dependent on glycine concentration (VERIFIED distinct form).** Confounders: glycine, which is itself elevated in PA (ketotic hyperglycinemia), and\[10\] GLYAT preferentially conjugates benzoyl-CoA over short-chain acyl-CoAs, so propionyl-CoA is a comparatively poor substrate.

### 3. Does C3/C2 track the mitochondrial propionyl-CoA/acetyl-CoA ratio?
- **Mechanistic basis (VERIFIED).** CrAT interconverts short-chain acyl-CoAs and their carnitine esters near equilibrium. Reported equilibrium constants:\[13\] CrAT "interconverts acetyl-CoA and acetyl-carnitine with an equilibrium constant of 1.5-1.8" (J Lipid Res, DOI 10.1194/jlr... in pending table); a cardiac hyperpolarised 13C study gives K = 0.6 and concludes "the maintenance of near-equilibrium in the CAT reaction ... suggests both high enzyme activity and a role for acetylcarnitine as an acetyl-CoA buffer" (PMC3378498). Because both C2 and C3 esters are set by the same near-equilibrium enzyme against a common free-carnitine and free-CoA pool, the C3/C2 ratio approximates the mitochondrial propionyl-CoA/acetyl-CoA ratio, with free carnitine largely cancelling.
- **Direct PA evidence (VERIFIED).** Baek 2024 (Nat Commun 2024;15:3804, DOI 10.1038/s41467-024-47460-9) states Pcca-/- (A138T) mice show "increases in the ratio of propionylcarnitine to acetylcarnitine (C3/C2)"\[14\] and that "propionyl-CoA competes with acetyl-CoA in a reaction mediated by carnitine acetyltransferase (CrAT), resulting in the production of propionylcarnitine (C3 AC) and acetylcarnitine (C2 AC)." The fasting study (Commun Biol 2024, DOI 10.1038/s42003-024-06362-8, PMC11137003) shows fasting drove\[15\] "a 62% decrease" in plasma C3/C2, "primarily driven by the substantial decrease in C3 levels,"\[16\] demonstrating that the ratio moves with the acetyl-CoA/propionyl-CoA balance and with nutritional state.
- **Caveat.** The ratio's dependence on acetyl-CoA (hence feeding/fasting and gluconeogenic state) is exactly what makes it informative as a second, differently-shaped observable, but it also means it is confounded by nutrition; sampling must be standardized.

### 4. Marker responses to mRNA-3927
- **Human phase 1/2 (VERIFIED, direction; SECONDARY, magnitude).** Nature 2024 (DOI 10.1038/s41586-024-07266-7, PMC11156579): "Reductions from baseline in disease biomarkers, including 3-HP, 2-MC, propionylcarnitine (C3) and n-propionylglycine (n-PG), were observed after dose 3 with mRNA-3927 in most patients (Extended Data Table 3)."\[11\] On clinical events, the relative risk for MDEs "was reduced by 70% during the treatment period of the dose-optimization study and by 82% among the biweekly treatment cohorts," and among only the cohorts dosed every two weeks the overall relative risk of MDEs in the treatment period versus the pretreatment period was 0.18 (95% CI, 0.011-2.907; P = 0.1832); across both studies a total of 346 intravenous doses were administered over 15.69 person-years, in 16 participants across 5 dose levels (cohort 1, n = 4; cohorts 2-5, n = 3 per group), with TEAEs in 15/16 (93.8%) and mRNA-3927-related TEAEs in 9/16 (56.3%). A Moderna 2023 investor deck (SECONDARY, not peer-reviewed) shows cohort-level steady-state 3-HP changes of -19% and -34% and states "7/8 patients showed a numerical reduction in 3-HP" with "a greater decline from baseline at 0.45 mg/kg,"\[17\] suggesting a dose response. Dose selection modeling (Attarwala/scienceopen, DOI 10.70534/JVQU8233) supports 0.6 mg/kg every 2 weeks.\[18\]
- **Mouse (Baek 2024, VERIFIED).** In Pcca-/- (A138T) mice "plasma 2-MC, 3-HP, and C3/C2 ratios were reduced for up to 3 to 4 weeks following the first IV dose."\[14\] The translational PK/PD model estimated\[12\] the liver PCC protein needed for 50% inhibition of plasma 2-MC, 3-HP, and C3/C2 as 21.0, 37.5, and 32.1 µg/g respectively, and near-maximal suppression was maintained at q3W doses of 2 mg/kg.
- **Mouse PK/PD (Attarwala 2023, VERIFIED).** Nucleic Acid Ther (DOI 10.1089/nat.2022.0036) used\[18\] "an indirect inhibition Kin model"\[18\] for 3-HP and 2-MC; reported 3-HP baseline (3-HP_base) of 60.1 µM/L and IC50_3-HP of 37.5 µg/g,\[12\] confirming a saturable (inhibitory Emax) dose-response of the markers to restored PCC rather than a linear one.
- **Note on n-PG and C3/C2 as human endpoints.** Only 2-MC and 3-HP are named primary/secondary plasma endpoints in the registered protocol (NCT04159103). C3 and n-PG were monitored; C3/C2 as a ratio is reported mainly in the mouse work. So among markers with a distinct functional form, C3/C2 has strong preclinical but weaker registered-human evidence, while 2-MC has both.

## Recommendation: which second marker adds independent information
Add the **C3/C2 acylcarnitine ratio** as the primary second marker, with **2-MC** as the secondary choice.
- **Why C3/C2.** Its value is set by a near-equilibrium enzyme (CrAT) and therefore reports the propionyl-CoA/acetyl-CoA ratio, a fundamentally different observable from a single-pool overflow marker like 3-HP. In identifiability terms, 3-HP is (approximately) a monotone function of the propionyl-CoA pool alone, whereas C3/C2 is a monotone function of a ratio; two observers that are monotone in different combinations of the latent states are not scaled copies and can jointly constrain theta plus a nuisance state (acetyl-CoA / carnitine). It was already measured in the mouse program with a fitted IC50, and it partially cancels the free-carnitine confounder that plagues absolute C3.
- **Why 2-MC as backup.** 2-MC formation is bilinear in propionyl-CoA and oxaloacetate, so it encodes TCA/anaplerotic state. This is the change the paper itself anticipated ("a marker that is the substrate"): 2-MC is the closest routinely-measured proxy that departs from strict proportionality to the propionyl-CoA pool. It is a named human trial endpoint, which C3/C2 is not, so it is the more defensible choice if only registered endpoints are admissible. Pairing 3-HP with 2-MC, and correcting 2-MC by citrate as the MCA/CA ratio (Sci Rep 2019), would further separate the propionyl-CoA signal from the oxaloacetate confounder.
- **Why NOT simply add C3 or n-PG alone.** Absolute C3 is a carnitine-scaled near-copy of the propionyl-CoA pool and adds mostly noise averaging, not a new functional form. n-PG has a genuinely distinct (sigmoidal, glycine-dependent) form but propionyl-CoA is a poor GLYAT substrate and glycine is itself disease-perturbed, so n-PG is expected to be the noisiest and least reliable second channel.

## Caveats and what remains unknown
- **Repository access (process caveat).** The target repository `guidoputignano/system-identification-pa` is private/not publicly indexed; it could not be cloned or read, so the specific files named in the task (00_abstract.tex, OPEN_QUESTIONS.md section 0, the "A marker panel" remark in 04_local.tex, the CV row in params.py, PHASE6_REVIEWS sections 11-12, 05_breath_test_repeatability.md, refs.sources, REVIEW_LOG R4, and the literature_pending.md template) could not be verified verbatim, and a git branch/PR could not be opened. This report supplies the full content intended for `refs/reviews_2026-10/03_markers.md` so it can be committed by someone with write access. The exact column layout of the pending-reference table below is a reasonable reconstruction and should be reconciled with the real `refs/literature_pending.md` before commit.
- **The central gap is unfilled.** There is still no primary within-patient CV for plasma 3-HP in PA in any matrix (plasma, DBS, urine) at any timescale (same-day, day-to-day, week-to-week). The honest input for the design is "none"; the defensible surrogate is roughly 25 to 35% total within-patient CV, extrapolated from between-visit serum MMA in MMA (31.38 ± 24.55%, Manoli 2021) and daily within-individual C3 in healthy adults (26.4%, Thompson 2012). Both surrogates are cross-disease or cross-population and should be flagged as such.
- **Kinetic forms for the human enzymes are partly inferred.** Human 2-MC synthase Km values are taken from the citrate-synthase side reaction and from fungal methylcitrate synthase, not from a human 2-MC-specific assay (none found). The linearity of the 3-HP overflow route at pathological propionyl-CoA is asserted from pathway structure, not from a measured human rate-versus-substrate curve (NOT FOUND).
- **C3/C2 as a human endpoint.** Its equilibrium-ratio behaviour is well supported mechanistically and in mice, but it is not a registered human trial endpoint and its nutrition dependence requires standardized sampling; a within-patient CV for C3/C2 in PA was NOT FOUND.
