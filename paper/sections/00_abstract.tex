\begin{abstract}
\noindent
The clinical decision in mRNA enzyme replacement for propionic acidemia is a dosing
interval, set by a quantity nobody measures: residual enzymatic capacity $\theta$. The
trial already runs the experiment that would identify it: the therapy is a known
exogenous input acting selectively on $\theta$, supplying $\num{excitation_ratio}$ times the excitation
the marker's own band does.

What transfers is the design rules. Sampling must cover the phase of the
dose cycle: draws at the dose interval, or a multiple of it, cost a factor of
$\num{alias_cost}$ against monthly sampling, and moving them half a day off the dose day
recovers it. The
dosing record is a regressor, not a nuisance. Intake must be
recorded as eaten and to better than about $\num{load_accuracy_gate}\%$, a requirement on
the record's \emph{accuracy}, not its variability. No prior on $K_m$ is needed.

Those rules hold at every capacity. The estimate does not, for one line of the balance:
what the record carries about $\ln\theta$ is the flux through the enzyme,
$\theta\,g(m) = L - k_e m$, so its sensitivity to capacity is capacity's own share of the
load: $\num{share_nominal_pct}\%$ at normal, $\num{share_5pct}\%$ at the $5\%$ that
defines the disease.
The band in which $\theta$ is recoverable at all is $\num{band_lo_pct}\%$ to
$\num{band_hi_pct}\%$ of normal, and propionic acidemia sits a factor of
$\num{disease_factor}$ below it. Fifteen times the sampling moves its lower edge by
$\num{band_dense_gain_pct}\%$: the limit is the identity, not the schedule. What
survives there is $K_m$, to $6$--$7\%$ at every capacity, and an upper bound on $\theta$.
The estimate is relative to an assumed structure too: under a published alternative
$\theta$ is not identifiable at all, at a residual too small to notice.

Identification is not what binds the decision anyway, the threshold being stipulated
rather than measured. The paper ends in a data specification: the first tier is one unpublished
measurement, the same-day repeatability of a $1$-$^{13}$C-propionate breath test; the
second is the non-enzymatic clearance $k_e$, which no published source supplies.
\end{abstract}
