\begin{abstract}
\noindent
The clinical decision in mRNA enzyme replacement for propionic acidemia is a dosing
interval, set by a quantity nobody measures: residual enzymatic capacity $\theta$. The
trial is already running the experiment that would identify it: the therapy is a known
exogenous input acting selectively on $\theta$, supplying forty-two times the excitation
the marker's own excursions do.

What transfers is the design rules that follow. Sampling must cover the phase of the
dose cycle: draws at the dose interval, or any multiple of it, cost a factor of
$\num{alias_cost}$ in precision, and moving the schedule half a day recovers it. The
dosing record is a regressor carrying the excitation, not a nuisance. Intake must be
recorded as eaten and to better than about $\num{load_accuracy_gate}\%$, a requirement on
the record's \emph{accuracy} and not its variability. No prior on $K_m$ is needed.

Those rules hold at every capacity. The estimate does not, for one line of the balance:
what the record carries about $\ln\theta$ is the flux through the enzyme,
$\theta\,g(m) = L - k_e m$, so its sensitivity to capacity is capacity's own share of the
load, $85\%$ at normal and $16\%$ at the $5\%$ of normal that defines the disease.
Monthly draws for two years therefore identify $\theta$ only between about $82\%$ and
$627\%$ of normal, and propionic acidemia sits a factor of $16$ below that band. What
survives there is $K_m$, to about $6\%$ at every capacity, and an upper bound on $\theta$.
The estimate is relative to an assumed structure too: under a published alternative
$\theta$ is not identifiable at all, at a residual too small to notice.

Identification is not what binds the decision in any case, the threshold being stipulated
rather than measured. The paper therefore ends in a data specification, whose first tier
is one unpublished measurement, the same-day repeatability of a
$1$-$^{13}$C-propionate breath test across a single dose, and whose second is the
non-enzymatic clearance $k_e$, which no published source supplies.
\end{abstract}
