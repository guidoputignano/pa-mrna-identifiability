\section{Reproducibility}
\label{sec:repro}
%==========================================================================

No figure, table or number in this note is typed by hand. Everything under
\texttt{results/} is regenerated from \texttt{src/}, and every structural claim is asserted
in \texttt{tests/test\_models.py}, so a parameter change that breaks a claim fails a test
rather than silently moving a figure.

\paragraph{What produces what.}
\begin{center}
\footnotesize
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{module} & \textbf{what it produces} \\
\midrule
\texttt{src/common/models.py}     & the kinetics, the three clearance structures, the equilibria \\
\texttt{src/common/params.py}     & Table~\ref{tab:params}, generated from the dataclasses \\
\texttt{src/common/dynamics.py}   & the sensitivity system and the observability Gramian \\
\texttt{src/common/excitation.py} & $\varphi$, $E$, $E_{\max}$, and the design quantities \\
\texttt{src/model\_C/run.py}      & the dynamic model and its equilibrium reduction \\
\texttt{src/model\_A/run.py}      & the local identification model, the fibre, the margin \\
\texttt{src/model\_B/run.py}      & the ceiling hypothesis of Remark~\ref{rem:ceiling} \\
\texttt{src/design/run.py}        & Sections \ref{sec:designsec} and \ref{sec:spec}, and Table~\ref{tab:spec} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\paragraph{Seeds and determinism.} Every stochastic result uses
\texttt{numpy.random.default\_rng} with an explicit seed, named at the call site. The
errors-in-variables simulation uses seeds $3$ and $7$; the decision operating characteristic
uses $11$ for the figure and $12$, $13$ and $21$ for the numbers quoted in
Section~\ref{sec:decision} and Table~\ref{tab:spec}. Fitted ensembles are cached on
$(\text{design}, \sigma, \mathrm{CV}, \text{reps}, \text{seed})$, so the same design is
fitted once and reused across an amplitude sweep; nothing in the sweep depends on the
illness amplitude except the margin evaluated afterwards.

\paragraph{Integration.} Euler appears in this note only for exposition. Every computed
trajectory uses an adaptive stiff-capable solver (\texttt{LSODA} or \texttt{Radau}) with
relative tolerances at $10^{-8}$ or tighter, and the estimator of
Section~\ref{sec:collapse} does not integrate at all: it uses the closed-form quasi-steady
level, whose validity is measured rather than assumed. Two tests guard this. One halves the
integration step and asserts the answer does not move by more than $10^{-6}$ relative; the
other asserts that an integrated steady state equals the closed-form equilibrium reduction
to the same tolerance.

\paragraph{The observation model is on the log scale.} A constant coefficient of variation
is a constant variance there, which is what gives $\sigma$ unambiguous units. This is a
structural fix rather than a preference: the one unit error this repository has made twice
was a percentage used as an absolute flux, and a log-scale observation model makes that
error unrepresentable.

\paragraph{The claim register.} Every factual assertion has a row in
\texttt{paper/claims.csv} naming its source and whether the source says what the assertion
says. \texttt{make audit} fails while any row is contradicted or unsourced, any citekey is
missing from the bibliography, or any \texttt{\textbackslash cite} in the paper has no row.
No citation in this repository is typed by hand: \texttt{paper/refs.bib} is fetched from
CrossRef and PubMed by \texttt{tools/fetch\_refs.py} from a file of identifiers.
