\noindent
This document holds the material that is not a link in the chain from the target
quantity to the data specification: the biological background, the full dynamic model
and its equilibrium reduction, the local model's derivation, the validation protocol,
the figures behind the results, and the open questions. Nothing here was deleted from
the paper; it was moved, and every cross-reference into the paper still resolves.

\emph{Section numbers in this document carry an} S. \emph{References to numbered results
without an} S \emph{point into the paper.}
