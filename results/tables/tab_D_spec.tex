\textbf{TIER 1} --- run first, and it is one draw &  &  \\
what to measure & two or three plasma draws inside the first half hour after one scheduled infusion, at the assay precision assumed throughout & rules out the published alternative and any structure whose marker has no relaxation of its own, under which $\theta$ is not identifiable at any design; see Section~\ref{sec:tierstruct} \\
what it must reach & a draw within fifteen minutes of the dose; the separation is $16.3\sigma$ at three minutes and $0.68\sigma$ at one hour & the two structures share the steady map exactly and differ only in the travel between levels, so the whole of the test is in the first hour \\
\textbf{TIER 2} --- conditional on Tier 1 &  &  \\
what to measure & same-day repeatability of $1$-$^{13}$C-propionate oxidation: two breath tests bracketing one dose, on one day & the only quantity the design turns on, and no published value for it exists \\
what it must reach & a coefficient of variation on $\Delta$ below $11.6\%$, $11.4$ to $11.9$\% across 24 records & derived from the gate, not chosen: at that precision monthly sampling reaches $20\%$ on $\theta$. This is a \emph{requirement}, so it is quoted as a median over intake records rather than as one draw \\
what is known now & between-visit CV, mean $14.0\%$, range $0.4$--$50.8\%$, $n=15$, intervals $2$ months to $4$ years & an upper bound on same-day error, already close to the requirement, but it does not establish it; and it is MMA, not PA \\
\textbf{TIER 3} --- conditional on Tier 2 &  &  \\
marker & plasma 3-hydroxypropionate & endpoint association (RR $0.7$, $95\%$ CI $0.6$--$0.9$) and immunity to the carnitine sign reversal that disqualifies C3 \\
sampling schedule & monthly across dose cycles, $n \approx 25$ over two years ($0.1699$, $0.1684$ to $0.1709$ across 24 records); two-monthly also passes ($0.1876$, $0.1839$ to $0.1939$ across 24 records). These are \emph{sampling spreads} of $\ln\hat\theta$, not interval half-widths; see Remark~\ref{rem:curvature} & ordinary outpatient density; what matters is covering the phase of the dose cycle, not the sampling rate \\
sampling, the hard constraint & \textbf{never at the dose interval or a multiple of it}: fortnightly gives $4.5845$, $3.5050$ to $5.6362$ across 24 records against the monthly design's $0.1699$, a cost of $27$-fold ($21$ to $33$ across records); $14.5$-daily gives $0.1615$, $0.1611$ to $0.1618$ across 24 records, which recovers it & every draw lands at the same phase, the increment stops varying, and the therapy channel closes \\
sampling, the other exclusion & \textbf{do not put a draw taken within one hour of a dose into the steady-state fit}. Such a draw may be taken, and is the only window that distinguishes the two structures of Section~\ref{sec:structuredep}; it is excluded from THIS fit, not from the protocol & the marker is not at quasi-steady state there and a steady-state fit does not apply to it. That is a constraint on the estimator and not on the patient \\
dosing record & date, dose and product lot for every infusion & $\Delta(t_i)$ is a regressor, not a nuisance: it carries the excitation \\
load record, precision & ordinary outpatient \emph{variability} suffices ($0.1696$ at a $1\%$ day-to-day CV against $0.1701$ at $20\%$); what binds is the \emph{per-visit recording error}, and it is the row below. It is \textbf{not} the mean offset: $98\%$ of the degradation at a $20\%$ recording error is per-visit scatter and $2\%$ is offset, so a weighed-intake calibration of the mean is not a substitute for a repeated as-eaten record & the offset is the direction the fibre runs along, and a varying recorded load closes it from the data, leaving its prior inert \\
load record, variability & \textbf{record intake as eaten; do not smooth, average or impute} & a steady load is the exactly singular case of Proposition~\ref{prop:rank}. With the $5\%$ load prior in place that direction is filled by the prior and the answer barely moves; with no prior it is filled only by real variation ($\infty$ steady, $0.26$ at a $2\%$ CV, $0.17$ at $20\%$), so the variability is what makes the result independent of the prior \\
$k_e$ & a prior within a factor of $2.67$ ($2.65$ to $2.68$ across records) & \textbf{no published source supplies it}, so this is the one number the design asks for and cannot cite. It is also the cheapest second external measurement: tightening it is worth $2.1\times$ at nominal capacity and $6.9\times$ in the disease range, though even an exact $k_e$ leaves $0.388$  there \\
$K_m$ & nothing at all & every number here is unchanged by a $K_m$ prior twenty times looser, which is what Remark~\ref{rem:no_km} demanded \\
